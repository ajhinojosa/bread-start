Frequently Asked Questions

Delivery & Ordering

What areas do you deliver to?

We offer free local delivery within Bend and the immediate surrounding areas (Redmond, Sisters, Tumalo). If you're outside this area, please contact us to see if we can accommodate your location.

How does home pickup work?

After placing your order, you'll receive an email with our address and available pickup times. Pickups are typically available within 24-48 hours of ordering. We'll have your starter ready and waiting!

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are ready within 24-48 hours. We prepare each starter fresh and feed it right before packaging to ensure it arrives active and ready to use.

Can I order in advance for a specific date?

Absolutely! Just let us know your preferred delivery/pickup date when you order. We're happy to coordinate timing for special occasions or planned baking sessions.

Starter Care

How do I care for my sourdough starter?

Each starter comes with detailed care instructions. In short: keep at room temperature and feed daily, or refrigerate and feed weekly. It's more forgiving than you think!

What if I can't feed it right away?

No problem! Your starter arrives well-fed. You can refrigerate it immediately and it will be fine for 5-7 days before needing its first feeding.

What do I feed my starter?

All-purpose or bread flour works great. We recommend using filtered or bottled water. The feeding ratio is simple: equal parts starter, flour, and water by weight.

How long does starter last?

With proper care, sourdough starter can last indefinitely! Some bakers have starters that are decades old. As long as you feed it regularly, it will keep thriving.

Baking

I'm a complete beginner. Can I do this?

Absolutely! Sourdough has a reputation for being difficult, but it's actually quite forgiving once you get the hang of it. We include a simple beginner recipe and care instructions with every order.

When can I start baking after receiving my starter?

Your starter arrives active and ready! You can start baking the same day, though we recommend giving it one feeding and letting it peak (become bubbly and doubled in size) before using it for best results.

Do you offer baking classes or guidance?

While we don't currently offer formal classes, we're always happy to answer questions! Reach out anytime if you need troubleshooting help or baking advice.

About

What is Oregon cottage food law?

Oregon's cottage food law allows home-based food businesses to sell certain low-risk foods directly to consumers. All cottage food products are prepared in home kitchens that meet food safety standards.

Where does your starter come from?

Our original starter culture has been maintained in Central Oregon for over a year, fed daily with organic flour and filtered water. It's adapted beautifully to our high desert climate.

We'd love to hear from you! Feel free to reach out with any questions about ordering, starter care, or baking. We're here to help make your sourdough journey successful.
